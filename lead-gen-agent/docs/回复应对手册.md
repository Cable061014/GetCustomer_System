# 客户回复应对手册（路由器 B2B 外贸）

> 用途：客户回复开发信后，按回复类型复制对应模板，填数字即可回复。
> 注意：所有 `【】` 里的内容是占位符，发送前必须替换成真实数据。

---

## 场景 1：客户要资料 / 报价

**客户可能说**：`send me your catalogue and pricing` / `please send price list`

**你的回复模板**：

```
Hi 【对方名 or team】,

Thanks for your interest. Please find our product catalogue and wholesale
price list attached.

Quick summary:
- Main products: 【列出 2-3 个主打型号/系列】
- MOQ: 【如 50 units】
- Lead time: 【如 15-20 days after order confirmation】
- Certifications: 【如 CE / FCC / RoHS】

Could you share your target market and estimated monthly volume? That way I
can prepare the most suitable pricing for you.

Best regards,
Cable
Subai (速佰)
wucablehh061014@gmail.com
```

**关键点**：附件里放产品目录 + 报价单（见下方「附件准备」）。同时**反问对方采购量**，这是进入真实谈判的信号。

---

## 场景 2：客户问具体参数（MOQ / 认证 / 交期）

**客户可能说**：`what's your MOQ?` / `do you have CE certification?` / `what's the lead time?`

**你的回复模板**：

```
Hi 【对方名】,

Good questions. Here are the details:

- MOQ: 【如 50 units per model】
- Certifications: 【如 CE, FCC, RoHS — certificates available on request】
- Lead time: 【如 15-20 days for standard orders, samples in 5-7 days】
- Warranty: 【如 12 months】
- Payment terms: 【如 30% deposit, 70% before shipment / T/T】

Anything else you'd like to know?

Best regards,
Cable
Subai (速佰)
```

**关键点**：参数要**准确**，不要为了成交虚报。客户问参数说明已经比较有兴趣了。

---

## 场景 3：客户犹豫 / 已有供应商

**客户可能说**：`we already work with TP-Link` / `why should we switch?`

**你的回复模板**：

```
Hi 【对方名】,

Totally understand — TP-Link is a solid brand. Where we can add value:

- Pricing: as a factory-direct supplier, our wholesale pricing often leaves
  a higher margin for distributors.
- Flexibility: we offer OEM/ODM and white-label, so you can build your own
  line without competing directly on the TP-Link range.
- Support: smaller MOQs and faster turnaround than working with large brands.

If useful, I can share a sample quote so you can compare — no obligation.

Best regards,
Cable
Subai (速佰)
```

**关键点**：不贬低对手，只讲自己的差异化（价格/OEM/灵活度）。给「无义务比价」的台阶。

---

## 场景 4：客户明确拒绝

**客户可能说**：`not interested` / `we don't need this now`

**你的回复模板**：

```
Hi 【对方名】,

No problem, thanks for letting me know. If anything changes down the road,
feel free to reach out.

Wishing you all the best.

Cable
Subai (速佰)
```

**关键点**：礼貌收尾，不纠缠，留个开放口子。**千万不要再连发追问**（会被拉黑）。

---

## 场景 5：客户没回（2-3 天后 follow-up）

**你的跟进模板**：

```
Hi 【对方名】,

Just following up on my last email — I know inboxes get busy.

If you'd like, I can send over a quick one-page summary of our router range
and wholesale pricing. Would that be useful?

Best regards,
Cable
Subai (速佰)
```

**关键点**：follow-up 只发**一次**，语气轻松不施压。再没回就放下，不骚扰。

---

## 附件准备（客户要资料时立刻能发）

你需要提前准备好两个文件：

1. **产品目录（catalogue）**：一页纸，列出主打路由器型号 + 图片 + 核心参数
2. **报价单（price list）**：型号 + 批发价 + MOQ + 交期

> 这两个文件我可以帮你做模板，你填真实型号和价格。等你要的时候说一声即可。

---

## 核心原则（务必记住）

1. **回复要快**：外贸客户问报价，越快回复成交率越高，尽量 24 小时内回。
2. **数据要真**：MOQ、认证、交期、价格不能虚报，否则后续必然穿帮。
3. **反问采购量**：能问出客户「月采购量」的，才是真客户。
4. **不纠缠**：拒绝或两次没回，就停手。
5. **全程英文**：客户是英国公司，用英文沟通。
